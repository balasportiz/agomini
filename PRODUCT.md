# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Runners, families, volunteers, community members, and potential partners in and around Barasat who need trustworthy event details, category information, registration/results access, and a clear sense of Agomoni Run’s social purpose.

## Product Purpose

Agomoni Run 2.0 is the official public campaign and content platform for Barasat Runners’ 4 October 2026 event. It should turn attention into informed participation while communicating women’s safety, community strength, Bengal’s festive spirit, and the symbolic welcome of Maa Durga without inventing unconfirmed event claims.

## Brand Personality

Kinetic, resolute, communal. The experience should feel cinematic and emotionally charged but grounded in practical race information, local identity, and collective responsibility.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble an e-commerce storefront, generic beige landing-page template, luxury editorial pastiche, neon sports campaign, card-grid SaaS site, or fabricated social-proof funnel. Avoid product language, shopping interactions, repeated icon cards, excessive glass effects, decorative gradients, and unverified sponsors, counts, policies, or claims.

## Design Principles

1. Let real movement and community photography carry the emotional narrative.
2. Make every conversion path trustworthy: confirmed facts are explicit and unknown details remain clearly pending.
3. Preserve spacious cinematic pacing while keeping race-day information easy to scan.
4. Express Bengal and Agomoni through authored details, language, rhythm, and imagery rather than clichéd decoration.
5. Treat motion as storytelling: purposeful, performant, and never required to access content.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA expectations with semantic landmarks, logical headings, visible focus, keyboard-operable navigation and dialogs, sufficient contrast, descriptive alt text, 44px touch targets, and reduced-motion alternatives. Bengali display text must use verified glyph support, and no interaction may depend on color, hover, animation, or precise pointer control alone.
